ABOUT ME

More Than the Numbers

When most recruiters come across a math major, I have noticed they tend to assume I spend all of my time doing theory and proofs. And while I have definitely done my fair share of both, I am not just a math major. I am an Applied Mathematics major concentrating in Data and AI. I chose applied math because I have always been much more interested in what I can do with math rather than just computing it. There is something so satisfying to me about taking any problem and turning it into the way my brain works best: numbers.

That is a big reason SpaceX has always stood out to me, and why this position specifically caught my attention. The Supply Chain Systems Analyst role combines all the things I enjoy most, from data to business (I have a finance minor too!). I like the idea of not only finding trends in data, but figuring out why they are happening and then building something that makes the process better and easier for everyone to understand, no matter their background!

There is also a more personal reason I am drawn to SpaceX. I attend Florida Tech in Melbourne and have built my life here on the Space Coast. I get to see launches from where I live, and Cape Canaveral is part of the community I have come to call home. I want to stay on the Space Coast after graduating, and I would love for the analytical skills I have spent my undergraduate career developing to contribute to the launch operations happening just up the road.

I know I am early in my career, and I am not going to pretend I have already mastered every system or technology listed in the job description. What I can bring is a strong quantitative foundation, experience with Python and data analysis, exposure to both technical research and real-world operations, and a genuine willingness to learn the things I do not know yet. Between being an NCAA student-athlete, working in research, completing internships, and maintaining a demanding academic schedule, I know I would be an asset to your hardworking team.

I became an applied mathematician because I wanted to solve real problems, and I would love to do that for a company like SpaceX.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I would be incredibly excited for the opportunity to contribute to the Launch Supply Chain team at Cape Canaveral.
